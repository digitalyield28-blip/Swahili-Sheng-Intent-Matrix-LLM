# Swahili-Sheng-Intent-Matrix-LLM
Human-verified semantic and behavioral intent matrices for low-resource African language model fine-tuning.
Swahili & Sheng Behavioral Intent Dataset (Vol. 1)
What is this project?
Standard web-scraping bots always struggle to interpret real context in East African digital spaces. Most automated models completely misclassify everyday urban slang (Sheng) and blended regional Swahili, which creates huge blind spots for customer service chatbots, voice recognition tools, and local financial tech models.

Over at the African Cultural Retelling Lab (ACRL) here in Nairobi, I have been manually structuring and human-verifying a 100-row dataset to bridge this gap. Instead of standard vocabulary lists, this matrix turns complex local expressions into organized computational data.
The Dataset Structure
Each row in the final sheet tracks five clear parameters:
* Token_ID: A simple alphanumeric tracking label (e.g., ACRL-001)
* Raw_Input_String: The authentic street slang exactly as it is spoken
* Linguistic_Layer: The sub-dialect breakdown (Sheng, Swahili, or a blend)
* Literal_Semantic_Mapping: A context-rich translation into standard English
* Context_Weight: A numeric urgency scale from 0.0 to 1.0 mapping the user's emotional state or distress levels
